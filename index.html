Plan vs actual

Bars = plan, red line = actual, dashed purple = forecast from open orders; click a bar to open that period.

How to read the chartBars: plan (green = days already reported, amber = still to come), quantity at the foot of each bar. Red line: actual (pallet log Jan–Oct, Telegram reports from November). Dashed purple line: open orders still to make, as scheduled (November = weekly schedule W-45/W-46, the rest of each open order in December, spread over its working days). Dashed grey vertical line: last reported day. Bottom row: actual ÷ plan. Yearly shows months, Monthly weeks, Weekly days, Daily and Shift the two shifts (plan = half the day plan). DIFAPI (600 L containers) is left out of all unit totals.

6 · Manufacturing lead times

Manufacturing lead times · order lines (estimated from pallet log)

Estimated from the pallet log (pallets carry no order number). Lead time = order date to production completion, not factory processing time: (completion date − order date) ÷ 30, in months. Actual completion = the day the order line's quantity was completed in the pallet log (95% of the quantity counts as complete); actuals of a product are given to its orders oldest order first. Lines not finished yet use the last scheduled forecast day (Forecast completion, lead time marked ~); a forecast day already past is shown as Due, not reported, until the daily reports arrive. Average rows (weighted by quantity, completed lines) after each product, then per client and for all products. Whole order book, not filtered by period.
